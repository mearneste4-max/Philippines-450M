## Slide 1

【提示】开场 30 秒。先谢对方时间；明确今天是技术交流、不是提案。把"你们已经拿到频谱、我们谈怎么用"这句立住。

Thank you for the time today. Globe has already answered the commercial question - you hold 450 MHz spectrum. What we want to discuss is what to do with it. This is a working session, not a proposal. I will take about fifteen minutes across five sections: the case for the band, the six industries it serves, the end-to-end architecture, the reference cases, and the business model. Please interrupt at any point.

---

## Slide 2

【提示】不用念数字。手势点地图，落点在最后一句：缺的不是速度，是灾后还站着的覆盖。停一秒再翻页。

Start with the geography, because it shapes everything. Seven thousand six hundred and forty-one islands, much of it mountainous. First in the World Risk Index, and around twenty tropical cyclones entering the area of responsibility every year. That has a specific consequence for network planning: the public network tends to fail at exactly the moment it is needed most. What is missing is not speed. It is coverage that is still standing after the storm.

---

## Slide 3

【提示】目录页，二十秒带过。重点是让对方知道后面怎么走，尤其是"六个行业"和"商业模式"两段。

Five sections. The first two are the argument - why this band, and what six industries do with it. The last three are the delivery - the architecture, the cases, and the business model. I will keep the industry section to one page each, so we can spend the time on the model at the end.

---

## Slide 4

【提示】这一页回答"这是不是只有我们在说"。三个数字按顺序念，第三个是预测口径，要说明是连接数不是网络数。

The world has settled on what this band is for. Eighty-six percent of the world's population lives where the 400 MHz bands are allocated or under consultation. Thirty-nine countries have already deployed networks, out of seventy-seven with allocations. The forecast is one point eight million broadband and fourteen million sensor connections by 2030. On our side: the Alliance lists Hytera in both its device and macro eNB supplier lists, and our handheld is through 450connect acceptance.

---

## Slide 5

【提示】全场技术核心。只念两个数：740 MHz 剩 91%，2.35 GHz 剩 59%。然后主动划边界，不要等客户问"那容量呢"。

Low-band physics is the whole argument. Reuse one grid at a higher frequency and part of the area is left uncovered: ninety-one percent at 740 megahertz, fifty-nine percent at 2.35 gigahertz. Closing that gap costs two and a half to three times the sites. What it buys is diffraction around terrain, deep penetration, and a site count small enough to afford backup power. What it does not buy is capacity: at three to five megahertz, this is a voice, telemetry and low-rate video layer.

---

## Slide 6

【提示】这一页专门补 MCS 的概念——后面的案例全是 MCS，不先讲清楚听众会断线。控制在四十秒。

Before the industries, one definition, because every case later runs on it. MCS is mission-critical services: MCPTT for push-to-talk voice with priority and pre-emption, MCVideo for live video from the scene, and MCData for messaging and location. It is defined by 3GPP, not by a single vendor, so the network stays multi-vendor and the terminals are not locked in. The division of labour is simple: 450 MHz gives the coverage, MCS gives the service.

---

## Slide 7

【提示】总览页，三十秒。六个行业各一句，不要展开——后面每页一个。

The same network serves six industries, on the same sites. Power and water come first because they pay for reliability over a ten to fifteen year horizon. Police and emergency add national value, and they need interoperability more than they need another network. Rail and port are the two shapes mid-band handles worst: a narrow corridor, and a run out to sea. Each of the next six pages takes one of them.

---

## Slide 8

【提示】电力是全球 450MHz 网络几乎都起步的地方。数字（121 家电合作社）一句带过，重点讲"付费能力"。

Power is where nearly every 450 MHz network starts, and it starts here for commercial reasons. One hundred and twenty-one electric cooperatives under NEA have assets spread across mountains and islands, they buy connectivity for ten to fifteen years, and they pay for reliability rather than speed. Three things run on the network: voice and lone-worker protection along the lines, a private data channel for SCADA and feeder automation, and smart meters on the same sites.

---

## Slide 9

【提示】水务的逻辑和电力一样，但卖点不同：把漏损"找出来"而不是"估出来"。这句要讲出来。

Water is the same commercial logic with a different payoff. Most water districts know their losses as a percentage, not as an address, because the meters and pressure sensors cannot report. On this network they can: metering and pressure sensing that locate losses instead of estimating them, pump and reservoir telemetry from remote and underground sites, and backup-powered sites that keep alarms flowing when the public network is down. One hundred and forty-nine water districts under LWUA.

---

## Slide 10

【提示】公共安全先讲"跨机构一次组呼"，不要讲设备。最后的新加坡是给对方的锚点，念慢一点。

For police, the requirement is not another private network - it is interoperability between the agencies that already have one. One group call across police, fire and civil defence. Live video from the scene, with priority and pre-emption when the network congests. And interworking with the DMR and TETRA networks already in service. Singapore has assigned 450 MHz band 31 to public safety, and it is in service today.

---

## Slide 11

【提示】应急是台风季的现实问题。强调"小时级部署"，不要说天。

Emergency is where this band earns its name in the Philippines. After a typhoon the public network fails exactly where the response is needed, and rapid response needs communications within hours, not days. That means portable base stations and satellite backhaul deployed quickly, a field command post that reaches the provincial EOC and the national agency, and sites that keep running on backup power when the grid does not.

---

## Slide 12

【提示】轨交讲"走廊型覆盖"，这是低 band 最擅长的形状。南非 Transnet 是锚点。

Rail is a narrow strip, often in cuttings and tunnels, and coverage has to follow the track rather than the population. That is one of the two shapes mid-band handles worst. What it needs is continuous coverage along the corridor, train-to-ground voice, data and video for operations and security, and it should run on the operator's network rather than a separate private system. South Africa has assigned the band to Transnet for exactly this.

---

## Slide 13

【提示】港口是群岛国家最现实的场景。港口页最容易讲得空，一定要落到"同一张网既管码头又管海上"。

Port is the most literal fit for an archipelago. Quay operations, ship-to-shore traffic and patrol boats all need the same group call, across water and steel. Port and terminal dispatch on one group call, ship-to-shore voice and data from coastal sites, and coast guard patrols covered far out to sea on the same network as the port. The same coastal sites also serve island and rural communities.

---

## Slide 14

【提示】架构页改成左右三栏。口吻中立：只说这一层有什么，不说"我们全都有"。三个箭头是层间接口。

One architecture, in three layers. At the terminal layer: the PNC660 450M handheld on bands 31 and 72, the MNC650 vehicle radio, a radio-style 5G model in the fourth quarter, and an ATEX variant next year. At the network layer: the 450 MHz macro eNB, a private or operator core, backhaul over fibre, microwave or satellite, and a rapid deployment kit. At the application layer: MCS services, dispatch, recording and device management.

---

## Slide 15

【提示】这一页是领导说"450M 案例不见了"要的那页。地图指一下，右侧名单挑三个念：德国、南非、新加坡。

This is the model working, market by market. Germany: 450connect holds band 72 and covers more than ninety-five percent of the country. Austria: ArgoNET, band 72. Poland: PGE and Polkomtel. Malaysia: TNB is procuring for four regions. Indonesia: SeaTelSat for rural connectivity. South Africa: band 31 allocated to Transnet for rail. And Singapore: band 31 for public safety, in service. In every one of them, a licensed operator carries the traffic and utilities anchor the demand.

---

## Slide 16

【提示】三个案例各一页。这一页最贴近运营商模式，重点不是数字，是"跑在运营商的公网上、不是自建专网"。

This is the closest match to your model. Sri Lanka Railways runs mission-critical services across nine lines and four hundred and three stations, with more than one thousand nine hundred and fifty terminals - on a public LTE network rather than a separate private system. The measured outcomes are worth noting: train delays down ten percent, management cost down sixty, track repair time down forty, and a landslide alert handled in thirty minutes.

---

## Slide 17

【提示】技术前沿页，也是把 450M 和 5G 的分工说清楚的地方：450M 管广覆盖，5G 管容量。

And the technical frontier. Hong Kong's Light Rail is the world's first railway MCS over 5G - eight hundred and ten terminals across handheld, bus-mounted and vehicle, with full redundancy including the wireless links. It replaced TETRA rather than overlaying it, and it shares the operator's own core network and radio access. That is where 450 megahertz and 5G complement each other, and it is the direction this market is moving.

---

## Slide 18

【提示】公共安全案例。说服力在"一次建成、两种用途"——日常调度和峰会安保。

A public safety case, on the same platform. Papua New Guinea's police headquarters runs its national command centre on Hytera ICC - three thousand officers and six hundred vehicles, city-wide video, and dispatch within sixty seconds of an alarm. It was also the security platform for the APEC summit: fifteen thousand foreign visitors and more than two hundred meetings, with automatic VIP route monitoring. One command centre, serving routine operations and a summit-level event.

---

## Slide 19

【提示】上一版这两页被指逻辑混乱，这版改成三方关系图。从左到右念一遍，重点是 Globe 的位置。

On the business model, three parties in one chain. Hytera supplies the radio, terminals, platform and integration. Globe holds the spectrum, builds and operates the network, and owns the customer relationship. The anchor customers - utilities, public safety, transport and ports - contract capacity before build-out. Three ways to cut the cost: reuse the towers, fibre, rooms and power Globe already has; take the site-count advantage of the low band; and contract the demand before the sites go up.

---

## Slide 20

【提示】收入页回答"这张网怎么赚钱"。六条线各一句，不要展开。最后一句是这一段的收口。

Six revenue streams on one build. IoT connectivity charged per meter or sensor. MCX as a service, per user. Capacity packages reserved for anchor customers. Device as a service, billed monthly. Integration and operations for SCADA and smart-city projects. And emergency readiness, with a rapid deployment kit on standby. None of them depends on consumer take-up - and the utility contract funds the coverage that the other five then use.

---

## Slide 21

【提示】这一页要"轻"。反复强调我们要的很少、且不要求现在承诺建设。最后一句是全场最重要的收口。

What we would need to go further is small. The band and bandwidth, so the coverage and capacity modelling is exact. One candidate province, ideally in the typhoon belt. And introductions to one electric cooperative and one water district willing to trial. The output would be coverage, availability and cost data measured against the public network. We are not proposing a build at this stage.

---

## Slide 22

【提示】收尾三句，不重复细节。说完停住，把时间交给对方提问。第一个问题通常是频谱参数和造价——造价不要给数，回到试点设计。

Thank you. To summarise: 450 megahertz is a coverage and resilience layer, not a capacity play. Utilities anchor it commercially, and public safety, emergency, rail and port add national value on the same sites. MCS is the service layer on top, and we are already through operator acceptance in Germany. The next step is confirming the spectrum parameters and nominating a pilot region.

## 备用问答（不进备注，口头准备）

| 可能的问题 | 建议口径 |
|---|---|
| 带宽只有 3–5 MHz，够用吗 | 够 MCPTT 语音、遥测遥控、低码率视频与定位；不要拿它跟 4G/5G 比容量。这正是 Alliance 说的"outside the capacity game" |
| 覆盖一个省要多少站 | 不在会上给数。回到试点：用 Globe 自己的地形与链路预算算出来才有意义 |
| 投资多少 | 不给数。说明需要先定频谱参数与试点范围，再谈投资模型 |
| 为什么不用 700M 或 800M | 同网格复用时 740 MHz 只剩 91.3% 覆盖，补齐要 2.5–3 倍站址；且 450M 是 Alliance 点名的全球通用低 band |
| MCS 和 PoC 有什么区别 | PoC 是运营商公网上的语音集群，MCS 是 3GPP 标准的任务关键型业务，含优先级、抢占、视频与数据。斯里兰卡和香港跑的都是 MCS |
| 你们在菲律宾做过 450M 吗 | 没有。但已有 DMR/TETRA 存量（公安、消防、铁路、民防），且德国的 450connect 白名单是可验证的运营商验收 |
| 和华为/中兴比 | 不评价友商。只讲 Alliance 设备报告中同时列入设备与宏基站的供应商很少 |
