**一句话主线**：450MHz 不是容量层，是覆盖与韧性层；先由付费能力最强的行业客户锚定需求，再由运营商把它建成一张网。

**One-line thread**: 450 MHz is not a capacity layer — it is a coverage and resilience layer. Demand is anchored first by the industries that pay for reliability, and the operator builds one network underneath them.

### P5 ｜ 为什么是 450MHz：物理层面 ｜ Why 450 MHz: the physics

**中文**｜全场技术核心。用"同一块面积、同一张网格"的示意图说明：450MHz 一个站覆盖，740MHz 要 2.5–3 倍站，2350MHz 要 12–15 倍。**主动划边界**——不要等客户问"那容量呢"。

**English**｜
- Coverage diagram: 1 site / 2.5–3× sites / 12–15× sites for the same area
- 100% · 91.3% · 58.7% covered from the same grid

**What that buys**｜bends around ridges · reaches basements and tunnels · few enough sites that backup power is affordable

**Conclusion band**｜What it is not: at 3–5 MHz it carries voice, telemetry and low-rate video. It stays outside the capacity game.

---

### P6 ｜ MCS 是什么 ｜ What MCS means  ← **新增页**

**中文**｜**领导指出"通篇没提 MCS，但后面案例全是 MCS"。这一页专门补上。** 说清三件事：MCS 是 3GPP 的国际标准（不是某家的私有系统）；它包含语音、视频、数据三类业务；450MHz 负责承载，MCS 负责业务。这样后面的斯里兰卡、香港案例才有落脚点。

**English**｜
- **MCS / MCX** — Mission-Critical Services over LTE and 5G, defined by 3GPP
- **MCPTT** — mission-critical push-to-talk voice
- **MCVideo** — live video from the scene
- **MCData** — messaging, file and location data
- Why it matters: an open standard, so the network is multi-vendor and the terminals are not locked to one supplier
- How it maps onto the band: 450 MHz carries the traffic; MCS is the service layer on top

**Conclusion band**｜450 MHz gives the coverage; MCS gives the service. The two are specified together.

### P8 ｜ 电力 ｜ Power

**中文**｜付费能力最强、也是全球 450MHz 网络几乎都从这里起步的行业。讲三件事：线路巡检与人身安全、SCADA 数据通道、电表与配电自动化。

**English**｜
- **Pain point** — mountain and island lines have no reliable signal; feeder automation needs a private data path
- Wide-area push-to-talk, video and lone-worker alarms along the lines
- A private data channel for SCADA, feeder automation and substation control
- Smart meters on the same sites, with deep indoor coverage
- **In the Philippines** — 121 electric cooperatives under NEA

---

### P9 ｜ 水利 ｜ Water

**中文**｜和电力同一套逻辑，但重点在"把漏损找出来而不是估出来"。泵站、水井、水库的遥测大多在山区和地下，正好是低 band 的强项。

**English**｜
- **Pain point** — non-revenue water is estimated, not located; pump and reservoir sites sit in mountains and underground
- Meters and pressure sensors that locate losses instead of estimating them
- Pump, well and reservoir telemetry from remote and underground sites
- Backup-powered sites keep alarms flowing when the public network is down
- **In the Philippines** — 149 water districts under LWUA

---

### P10 ｜ 公安 ｜ Police

**中文**｜重点不是"再建一张专网"，而是"跨机构一次组呼"和"现场视频回传"。强调与既有 DMR/TETRA 互通，不推翻已有投资。

**English**｜
- **Pain point** — different agencies cannot talk to each other; the public network congests exactly when it is needed
- One group call across police, fire and civil defence
- Live video from the scene, with priority when the network congests
- Interworking with the DMR and TETRA networks already in service
- **Reference** — Singapore assigned 450 MHz band 31 to public safety

---

### P11 ｜ 应急 ｜ Emergency

**中文**｜灾害场景。讲"灾后还站着的覆盖"和"快速部署"：便携基站、卫星回传、现场指挥箱。这一页回答台风季的现实问题。

**English**｜
- **Pain point** — after a typhoon the public network is down exactly where the response is needed
- Rapid deployment: portable base stations and satellite backhaul within hours
- A field command post that reaches the provincial EOC and the national agency
- Coverage that survives on backup power when the grid does not

---

### P12 ｜ 轨交 ｜ Rail

**中文**｜走廊型覆盖是低 band 最擅长的形状之一，也是全球 450MHz 最成熟的行业应用之一。讲沿线连续覆盖、车地通信、与既有信号系统的关系。

**English**｜
- **Pain point** — a rail corridor is a narrow strip, often in cuttings where mid-band cannot follow
- Continuous coverage along the corridor, including cuttings and tunnels
- Train-to-ground voice, data and video for operations and security
- Keeps running on the operator's network rather than a separate private system
- **Reference** — South Africa assigned the band to Transnet for rail

---

### P13 ｜ 港口 ｜ Port  ｜ **新增行业页**

**中文**｜群岛国家最现实的场景：港区、近海、海岸警卫队。讲港区调度、船岸通信、以及"从岸基站覆盖到多远的海面"。

**English**｜
- **Pain point** — port areas are dense and metallic; coast guard patrols run far beyond mid-band reach
- Port and terminal dispatch on one group call across the quay
- Ship-to-shore voice and data from coastal sites
- Coast guard patrols covered far out to sea, on the same network as the port

### P14 ｜ 端到端：终端 — 网络 — 应用 ｜ End to end: terminal – network – application

**中文**｜**改成左右三栏，不再是上下堆叠。口吻改中立**：不写"one supplier"这类推销话，只客观说明这一层是什么、由哪些设备构成。底部一条色带说明同一套架构服务六个行业。**图片不再只拼三张产品照**，每栏配该层的代表图（终端实物、基站、调度界面）。

**English**｜
- **Terminal** — PNC660 450M handheld · MNC650 vehicle · P70 5G+450M · PNC660Ex ATEX
- **Network** — 450 MHz macro eNB · private core · fibre, microwave or satellite backhaul · rapid deployment kit
- **Application** — MCS voice, video and data (MCPTT / MCVideo / MCData) · dispatch · recording · device management
- Strip: the same architecture serves power, water, police, emergency, rail and port

### P19 ｜ 商业模式：谁做什么 ｜ Business model: who does what

**中文**｜**上一版这两页被指"逻辑混乱"，重做。** 改成一张三方关系图，从左到右一条线讲清楚：海能达供设备与平台 → Globe 持频谱、建网、运营 → 行业客户签约付费。下面三句话讲清各自的投入与所得。**删掉 410MHz 波兰那句**，全篇统一口径在 450MHz。

**English**｜
- Hytera — supplies the radio, terminals, platform and integration
- Globe — holds the spectrum, builds and operates the network, owns the customer relationship
- Anchor customers — utilities, public safety, transport, ports: contract capacity before build-out
- Three ways to cut the cost: reuse Globe's towers, fibre, rooms and power · far fewer sites for the same coverage · contract anchor demand before the sites go up

### P20 ｜ 收入从哪来 ｜ Revenue streams

**中文**｜一页讲清六条收入线，回答"这张网怎么赚钱"。

**English**｜
- IoT connectivity — monthly fee per meter or sensor
- MCX-as-a-Service — hosted push-to-talk, charged per user
- Capacity packages — reserved network share, fixed fee
- Device-as-a-Service — devices bundled, billed per month
- Integration & O&M — SCADA and smart-city projects
- Emergency readiness — rapid deployment on standby

### P21 ｜ 路线图：先试点，再铺开 ｜ Roadmap: pilot first, then scale

**中文**｜五步走，弱化"我们要卖什么"，强化"下一步只需要你们给什么"。

**English**｜
- 01 Confirm the spectrum — band, bandwidth, licence term
- 02 Pick a pilot region — one province, two anchor customers
- 03 Build the pilot — sites, platform, terminals, meters
- 04 Prove the case — coverage, availability and cost data
- 05 Scale nationwide — emergency, transport, smart city

**Conclusion band**｜We are not proposing a build. The next step is a joint pilot design.
