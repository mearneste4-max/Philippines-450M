## Slide 1

【提示】开场 30 秒。先谢对方时间，说明今天是技术交流、不是提案。把"你们已经有频谱，我们谈怎么用"这句话立住。

Thank you for your time today. Globe already holds 450 MHz spectrum, so the question is not whether to have it but what to do with it. This is a working session, not a proposal. I will give you the answer first, then why this band, who pays for it, and how it would be delivered. Please interrupt at any point.

---

## Slide 2

【提示】整篇最重要的一页。四行各念一句，最后落在底部的"今天要的决定"。VP 只记住这一页也够了。

Here is the answer on one page. What it is: a resilience layer, alongside your 4G and 5G, not a replacement. Why now: the band is mainstream, with networks in seventy-seven countries and thirty-nine already on LTE. Who pays first: the utilities, power and water. What we propose: one pilot province with two anchor customers. And what we would ask today is small: confirm the spectrum parameters and nominate a pilot province.

---

## Slide 3

【提示】国情页。不要逐个念数字，手指点 1.4M 那格：一次台风就有一百四十万用户断电。落点是底部两条色带的对比。

The geography explains the need. Seven thousand six hundred and forty-one islands, first in the World Risk Index, around twenty cyclones a year. Typhoon Tino alone left one point four million electricity consumers without power. Public 4G and 5G do the capacity job in the cities very well. What is missing is coverage that is still standing after the storm, and that is the job of a 450 MHz layer.

---

## Slide 4

【提示】技术核心页，只讲方格图：同一套站址，740 MHz 少 9%，2.35 GHz 少 41%。然后主动划边界：不做容量。

This is the physics in one picture. Same site grid, three frequencies. At 450 MHz the grid covers everything. At 740 megahertz about nine percent goes dark, at 2.35 gigahertz about forty-one percent. Closing those gaps costs two and a half to fifteen times the sites. Fewer sites is what makes backup power affordable. To be clear about the limit: with three to five megahertz this carries voice, telemetry and low-rate video. It is not a capacity layer.

---

## Slide 5

【提示】世界地图页。指深蓝色：大部分网络由电力公司锚定。再指菲律宾黄圈：Globe 持有的是 B31，和新加坡、马来西亚同一个频段。

The band is mainstream. Eighty-six percent of the world lives where it is allocated or under consultation, seventy-seven countries have networks, and thirty-nine already run LTE. The colours show what the networks are used for: dark blue is utilities and smart grid, which is the largest group. The pattern repeats everywhere: one licensed operator carries the traffic and the utilities anchor the demand. The Philippines licence is Band 31, the same band as Malaysia and Singapore.

---

## Slide 6

【提示】这页给立场。先说结论"先做公用事业"，再说明警察预算最大但变现最慢。最后一定要说"这是我们的判断，要在试点里验证"。

Six industries can use this network, but they do not pay at the same speed. We rank power and water first: they have the budget, the fit is strong, and revenue comes quickly. Emergency is next, paid as standby readiness. Police has the largest budget but the slowest path, because DMR and TETRA are already in service. Rail and port come later. This ranking is our reading, to be tested in the pilot.

---

## Slide 7

【提示】电力页，优先级第一。讲两件事：菲律宾有 121 家电合作社；德国和马来西亚的 450 网络都是电力公司锚定的。底部绿卡是 Globe 能卖的东西。

Power is the anchor customer. There are one hundred and twenty-one electric cooperatives under NEA, and a single typhoon cut off one point four million consumers. The network gives them three things: a private data path for SCADA and feeder automation, push-to-talk for line crews, and smart meters deep indoors. This is exactly how 450connect in Germany and TNB in Malaysia started. For Globe it is a capacity package, IoT per meter, and integration.

---

## Slide 8

【提示】水务页。卖点是"把漏损找出来，而不是估出来"。数字：532 个水务区里只有 149 个漏损达标。

Water uses the same sites as power with a different payoff. Of five hundred and thirty-two water districts, only one hundred and forty-nine kept their losses within LWUA's twenty percent limit. Most districts know their losses as a percentage, not as a location. On this network meters report, pressure sensors locate the leaks, and pump and reservoir sites send telemetry. Bahrain already runs a water-meter network in this band range.

---

## Slide 9

【提示】应急页，排第三。台风国情最直接对应这一页。收费模式是"备勤费"，不按用量。

Emergency is the industry that matches the typhoon risk most directly. When the public network fails, a 450 MHz layer with satellite backhaul can be extended within hours, with a field command post on the same network. In Indonesia, SeaTelSat is building Band 31 rural LTE over satellite backhaul. For Globe this is a readiness retainer: kits on standby, paid by the province or the agency whether or not a storm comes.

---

## Slide 10

【提示】警察页。口径是"互通，不是替换"。不点名任何菲律宾警务客户。

For police, the requirement is not another private network. It is one group call across agencies that already run DMR and TETRA, with priority when the public network congests. The network provides cross-agency group calls, live video from the scene, and interworking with the radio systems already in service. Singapore runs Band 31 for public safety today. For Globe it is MCX as a service, per user per month.

---

## Slide 11

【提示】铁路页。核心一句：覆盖要沿着铁轨走，而不是沿着城镇走。

Rail needs coverage that follows the track, not the towns, including cuttings and tunnels. A 450 MHz corridor gives dispatch along the line and on-board video on one network, rather than a separate private system. South Africa has assigned Band 31 to Transnet for rail, and our own MCS runs on nine lines in Sri Lanka. For Globe this is MCX as a service and devices as a service.

---

## Slide 12

【提示】港口页，排最后，坦白说没有 450 港口先例，论证靠"海面覆盖"。不要硬说有案例。

Port is the last on our list, and we will be candid: there is no 450 MHz port network to point to yet. The case rests on coverage over open water: quay dispatch, ship-to-shore voice and data, and coast guard patrols on one group call. The same coastal sites also serve island communities. For Globe it is MCX as a service plus capacity, if the pilot data supports it.

---

## Slide 13

【提示】架构页。一句话：同一张网服务六个行业，只换终端和应用。MCS 是 3GPP 标准，不锁定厂商。

One build serves all six industries. Only the terminals and the applications change. At the terminal layer: the PNC660 450M handheld, a vehicle radio, and a 5G plus 450 model coming in 2026. At the network layer: 450 MHz eNodeBs, a private or shared core, and backhaul. On top sit the mission-critical services. MCS is a 3GPP standard and runs on any LTE or 5G radio, so it carries over to 450 MHz unchanged.

---

## Slide 14

【提示】案例页分两行，三比三。上一行是在网运行的 450 MHz 运营商网络（不是我们建的，我们的关联是德国白名单）；下一行是海能达自己的 MCS 项目（跑在 LTE/5G 上）。这个区分一定要讲清。

Two kinds of evidence, three of each. Top row: 450 MHz networks in service. 450connect covers more than ninety-five percent of Germany, and our PNC660 is on its whitelist. TNB in Malaysia and Singapore's public safety network both run Band 31, the same band as yours. Bottom row: our MCS at national scale on LTE and 5G. Sri Lanka Railways with one thousand nine hundred and fifty terminals, Hong Kong Light Rail, the first railway MCS on 5G, and the Papua New Guinea police command centre.

---

## Slide 15

【提示】商业模式页。三方链条从左到右念一遍；六条收入线不展开，只说"每条都对应前面某个行业页"。最后讲葡萄牙教训：先签需求再建网。

Three parties, one chain. Hytera supplies the radio, terminals and platform. Globe holds the spectrum, runs the network and owns the customer. The anchor customers contract capacity first. That gives six revenue lines, each tied to an industry page you have seen. The lesson from Portugal is simple: the 450 MHz trial there stalled on commercial terms, not on technology. So the sequence matters: sign the demand before the build.

---

## Slide 16

【提示】下一步。强调"不是建网，是联合设计试点"。三项需求念一遍：频谱参数、一个候选省、两个引荐。

We are not proposing a build. We are proposing a pilot that tests the assumptions in your own terrain. Five steps: confirm the spectrum, pick a typhoon-belt province, build the pilot, measure coverage, uptime and cost, and then decide on scale. To start we need three things: the band and bandwidth, one candidate province, and introductions to one electric cooperative and one water district.

---

## Slide 17

【提示】收尾，复述三项下一步，然后开放讨论。

Thank you. To summarise the next step: confirm the spectrum parameters, nominate a pilot province, and design the pilot together. We are happy to take questions now, and to run a follow-up technical workshop with your network planning team.
