## Slide 1

【提示】开场 30 秒。说明今天是技术交流、一起探讨应用场景，不是提案。

Thank you for your time today. Globe already holds 450 MHz spectrum, so the interesting question is what it could be used for. Today is an exchange of ideas, not a proposal. We will look at four questions together, and please interrupt at any point.

---

## Slide 2

【提示】目录页。四章各念一句问题，强调"这些是一起探讨的选项"。

Four questions frame the discussion. First, what can this band do that mid-band cannot. Second, which industries need it, and who would pay first. Third, how such a network is built, and where it runs today. And fourth, how it could pay for itself. The spectrum is Globe's; these are options to explore together.

---

## Slide 3

【提示】第 1 章过渡页，一句话带过。

Let us start with the band itself: what can 450 megahertz do that mid-band cannot?

---

## Slide 4

【提示】合并页：左边是菲律宾的三个需求（广覆盖、跨山地、扛风暴），右边是方格图给出 450 的回答。只念两个数：740 MHz 少 9%，2.35 GHz 少 41%。最后主动划边界：不做容量。

The Philippines asks three things of a network: reach more than seven thousand islands, cross mountainous terrain, and stay up through around twenty cyclones a year. On the right is the same site grid at three frequencies. At 450 the grid covers everything; at 740 megahertz about nine percent goes dark, at 2.35 gigahertz about forty-one percent. Fewer sites also makes backup power affordable. It is not a capacity layer: three to five megahertz carries voice, telemetry and low-rate video.

---

## Slide 5

【提示】世界地图页。指深蓝色：大部分网络由电力公司锚定。再指菲律宾黄圈：Globe 持有的是 B31，和新加坡、马来西亚同一个频段。

The band is mainstream. Eighty-six percent of the world lives where it is allocated or under consultation, seventy-seven countries have networks, and thirty-nine already run LTE. The colours show what the networks are used for: dark blue is utilities and smart grid, which is the largest group. The pattern repeats: one licensed operator per country, and utilities are the largest group of users. The Philippines licence is Band 31, the same band as Malaysia and Singapore.

Second question: which industries need this network, and who would pay first?

## Slide 7

【提示】这页给立场。先说结论"先做公用事业"，再说明警察预算大但变现最慢。最后一定要说"这是我们的判断，想听听 Globe 的看法"。

Six industries can use this network, but they do not pay at the same speed. We rank power and water first: they have the budget, the fit is strong, and revenue comes quickly. Emergency is next, paid as standby readiness. Police budgets are large, but the path is slowest, because DMR and TETRA are already in service. Rail and port come later. This ranking is our reading, and we would like to test it against yours.

---

## Slide 8

【提示】电力页，优先级第一。讲两件事：菲律宾有 121 家电合作社；德国和马来西亚的 450 网络都是电力公司锚定的。底部绿卡是 Globe 能卖的东西。

Power is the anchor customer. There are one hundred and twenty-one electric cooperatives under NEA, and a single typhoon cut off one point four million consumers. The network gives them three things: a private data path for SCADA and feeder automation, push-to-talk for line crews, and smart meters deep indoors. This is exactly how 450connect in Germany and TNB in Malaysia started. For Globe it is a capacity package, IoT per meter, and integration.

---

## Slide 9

【提示】水务页。卖点是"把漏损找出来，而不是估出来"。数字：532 个水务区里只有 149 个漏损达标。

Water uses the same sites as power with a different payoff. Of five hundred and thirty-two water districts, only one hundred and forty-nine kept their losses within LWUA's twenty percent limit. Most districts know their losses as a percentage, not as a location. On this network meters report, pressure sensors locate the leaks, and pump and reservoir sites send telemetry. Bahrain already runs a water-meter network in this band range.

---

## Slide 10

【提示】应急页，排第三。台风国情最直接对应这一页。收费模式是"备勤费"，不按用量。

Emergency is the industry that matches the typhoon risk most directly. When the public network fails, a 450 MHz layer with satellite backhaul can be extended within hours, with a field command post on the same network. In Indonesia, SeaTelSat is sourcing equipment for Band 31 rural LTE over satellite backhaul. For Globe this is a readiness retainer: kits on standby, paid by the province or the agency whether or not a storm comes.

---

## Slide 11

【提示】警察页。口径是"互通，不是替换"。不点名任何菲律宾警务客户。

For police, the requirement is not another private network. It is one group call across agencies that already run DMR and TETRA, with priority when the public network congests. The network provides cross-agency group calls, live video from the scene, and interworking with the radio systems already in service. Singapore runs Band 31 for public safety today. For Globe it is MCX as a service, per user per month.

---

## Slide 12

【提示】铁路页。核心一句：覆盖要沿着铁轨走，而不是沿着城镇走。

Rail needs coverage that follows the track, not the towns, including cuttings and tunnels. A 450 MHz corridor gives dispatch along the line and on-board video on one network, rather than a separate private system. South Africa has assigned Band 31 to Transnet for rail, and our own MCS runs on nine lines in Sri Lanka. For Globe this is MCX as a service and devices as a service.

---

## Slide 13

【提示】港口页，排最后，坦白说没有 450 港口先例，论证靠"海面覆盖"。不要硬说有案例。

Port is the last on our list, and we will be candid: there is no 450 MHz port network to point to yet. The case rests on coverage over open water: quay dispatch, ship-to-shore voice and data, and coast guard patrols on one group call. The same coastal sites also serve island communities. For Globe it is MCX as a service plus capacity, if the data supports it.

---

## Slide 14

【提示】第 3 章过渡页。

Third question: how is such a network built, and where does it run today?

---

## Slide 15

【提示】架构页。一句话：同一张网服务六个行业，只换终端和应用。MCS 是 3GPP 标准，不锁定厂商。

One build serves all six industries. Only the terminals and the applications change. At the terminal layer: the PNC660 450M handheld, a vehicle radio, and a 5G plus 450 model coming in 2026. At the network layer: fixed eNodeBs on towers, mobile eNodeBs for rapid deployment, a private or shared core, and backhaul. On top sit the mission-critical services. MCS is a 3GPP standard and runs on any LTE or 5G radio, so it carries over to 450 MHz unchanged.

---

## Slide 16

【提示】王总要求加的一页。左边讲"我们是 450 生态的正式成员"（2024 年 8 月加入 Alliance、进了设备和宏基站两张供应商名单、PNC660 过了 450connect 白名单）。右边讲兼容：基于 3GPP 标准接口，可以接入 Globe 现有的华为、中兴等核心网。注意口径：只说"按标准设计、可接入"，具体互通在项目中逐一验证，不要说"已和华为核心网测试通过"，除非产品线给了测试记录。

We are part of the 450 MHz ecosystem, not a newcomer to it. Hytera joined the 450 MHz Alliance in 2024 and is on its supplier lists for both devices and macro base stations, and our handheld is accepted on 450connect's network in Germany. Just as important for Globe: everything is built on 3GPP standard interfaces, so our radio and terminals can plug into the core you already run, whether that is Huawei, ZTE, Nokia or Ericsson. Interoperability is verified for each project.

---

## Slide 17

【提示】案例页分两行，三比三。上一行是在网运行的 450 MHz 运营商网络（不是我们建的，我们的关联是德国白名单）；下一行是海能达自己的 MCS 项目（跑在 LTE/5G 上）。这个区分一定要讲清。

Two kinds of evidence, three of each. Top row: 450 MHz operator networks. 450connect covers over ninety-five percent of Germany, with our PNC660 on its whitelist. TNB in Malaysia is rolling out Band 31, and Singapore's public safety network runs on Band 31, the same band as yours. Bottom row: our own mission-critical deployments. Sri Lanka Railways with one thousand nine hundred and fifty terminals, Hong Kong Light Rail, the first railway MCS on 5G, and the Papua New Guinea police command centre.

## Slide 19

【提示】商业模式页。三方链条从左到右念一遍；六条收入线不展开，只说"每条都对应前面某个行业页"。最后讲葡萄牙教训：先签需求再建网。

Three parties, one chain. Hytera supplies the radio, terminals and platform. Globe holds the spectrum, runs the network and owns the customer. The anchor customers contract capacity first. Utilities fund the coverage, and the other industries build on it: six revenue lines, each tied to an industry page you have seen. The lesson from Portugal is simple: the 450 MHz trial there stalled on commercial terms, not on technology. So the sequence matters: contract the demand before the build.
